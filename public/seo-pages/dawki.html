Hotels, Homestays, Cottages & Rooms in Dawki

Dawki, Meghalaya. Dawki is a border-area destination in Meghalaya associated with the Umngot River and journeys through the Jaintia Hills. Conditions and visitor arrangements can vary by season.

Where to stay and how to plan

If Dawki is the main focus of your itinerary, compare staying nearby with travelling from Shillong or Jowai. Ask the host about road access, the distance to your planned activity and local transport availability.

Homestays and small guest properties may suit visitors looking for a local base, while facilities can vary. Confirm meals, hot water, parking, room capacity and check-in arrangements.

Questions to ask the property owner

  • What is the exact address and map location?
  • Do the photos show the actual room and facilities?
  • What is the total price, including taxes and deposits?
  • What are the check-in time, house rules and cancellation terms?
  • For monthly stays, what are the rent cycle, utilities and notice period?

Local travel notes

Check current border-area travel guidance, weather and road conditions through official or local sources. Confirm boat or activity operators separately; a room listing does not include those services unless explicitly agreed.

Property details and availability are supplied by owners and can change. A WhatsApp enquiry is not a confirmed reservation. Confirm dates, room type, total price and payment terms directly with the host. Do not assume every listing has been independently inspected.
