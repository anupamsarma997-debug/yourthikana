Gangtok Hotels, Homestays & Rooms

Gangtok, Sikkim — destination guide and stay discovery on YourThikana.

Gangtok is the capital of Sikkim and a popular starting point for exploring the state's mountain scenery, monasteries and local culture. MG Marg is a well-known pedestrian area, while Enchey Monastery is a recognised cultural site. Travellers also plan trips beyond the city, subject to weather, permits and local conditions.

Think about the area you want to stay in, transport access, guest count and length of visit. Ask the owner for the exact address, check-in arrangements, room facilities and total cost. For destinations such as Tsomgo Lake and other restricted areas, confirm permit rules and road conditions through official sources before travelling.

YourThikana helps travellers discover accommodation listings and contact hosts directly on WhatsApp. Browse the Gangtok listings view to see options published by property owners. Confirm dates, availability, price, included services and cancellation terms with the host before paying. A listing is not a confirmed reservation, and prices or availability may change.
