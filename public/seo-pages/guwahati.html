Guwahati is a major city in Assam on the banks of the Brahmaputra River and an important starting point for journeys across Northeast India. Visitors come for the city's riverfront, markets, cultural sites and transport connections. Kamakhya Temple is one of the best-known places in the city, while Umananda Temple sits on Peacock Island in the Brahmaputra. A visit can also include local food, museums and neighbourhood markets.

When planning a stay in Guwahati, choose an area based on your travel purpose. A short city visit, a railway or airport connection, a family trip and a longer work stay can call for different locations and room types. Check the exact address, transport options, check-in time, guest capacity and facilities with the property host before confirming.
