How to choose where to stay

Start with the main reason for your visit. If you have appointments or sightseeing plans in several parts of the city, compare the property's location with your daily route. If you are arriving by air, rail or road, ask the host about the practical journey from your arrival point and the transport options available at your expected arrival time.

Guwahati is also a base for visitors travelling to places around Assam and the wider Northeast. If your plan includes Kamakhya Temple or other destinations, check the route and expected travel time from the property's exact address rather than relying only on the city name shown in a listing.

For a monthly stay, prioritise the exact neighbourhood, access to work or study, kitchen and laundry arrangements, water supply, electricity, internet, visitor rules and the full monthly cost. A low advertised rent may not include every recurring charge.

What to check before contacting a host

  • Exact property address or map location, not only “Guwahati”.
  • Room type, number of beds, maximum guest capacity and whether the photos show the actual room.
  • Check-in and check-out times, ID requirements and house rules.
  • Total price for your dates, including taxes, extra guest charges, deposit or other fees.
  • Facilities that matter to you, such as air conditioning, hot water, Wi-Fi, parking, meals or lift access.
  • For monthly rooms: rent cycle, security deposit, electricity and water charges, notice period and refund terms.
  • Cancellation and refund conditions, agreed in writing before sending money.

Contact property owners directly

When a listing provides a WhatsApp contact option, use it to ask the owner questions and confirm the details. Keep a written record of the agreed dates, room, total price and payment terms. Do not send an advance payment until you are satisfied that the property and contact details are genuine. If anything seems suspicious or a listing appears misleading, use the website's report option where available.

Important: YourThikana supports direct enquiries; a WhatsApp message is not a confirmed booking. Property information, prices and availability are supplied by owners and can change. Do your own checks and confirm every booking term directly with the property.
