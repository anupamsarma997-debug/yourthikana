Hotels, Homestays, PGs & Rooms in Imphal

Imphal, Manipur — Imphal is the capital and main urban centre of Manipur. It is the principal base for many visitors arriving for work, education, family visits and city-based itineraries.

Choosing an area and accommodation in Imphal

Choose accommodation according to your destination within the city and the transport you will use. If travelling outside Imphal, check current local travel advice and route conditions rather than assuming the city is a convenient base for every district.

Hotels may suit short visits, while PGs, hostels and monthly rooms may suit students or longer stays where owners have listed them. Confirm the room type and occupancy directly.

What to confirm before contacting a host

  • Exact property address and map location.
  • Actual room photos, room type, bed count and guest capacity.
  • Check-in and check-out time, house rules and ID requirements.
  • Total price, taxes, deposit, extra guest fees and cancellation terms.
  • Facilities such as Wi-Fi, hot water, parking, meals and power backup.
  • For monthly rooms: rent cycle, utilities, security deposit and notice period.

Local travel and planning notes

Ask for the exact address, current access information, check-in rules, total price and payment terms before sending money.

Property details, prices and availability are provided by owners and can change. A WhatsApp enquiry is not a confirmed reservation. Keep a written record of the agreed dates, room, total price and payment terms; do not pay until you are satisfied that the property and contact details are genuine.

Important: YourThikana helps guests discover owner-published accommodation and contact hosts directly. The platform does not claim that every property has been independently inspected or that a room is available until the host confirms it.

Frequently asked questions

What accommodation can I look for in Imphal?

Depending on owner-published listings, you can look for hotels, homestays, cottages, hostels, PG accommodation and monthly rooms.

How do I check a property's location in Imphal?

Ask the host for the complete address or map pin and compare it with your planned route before confirming.

Does a WhatsApp enquiry confirm my stay?

No. Confirm dates, room type, total price, payment terms and cancellation conditions directly with the property owner.

Are all listings independently inspected?

Do not assume every listing has been independently inspected. Review the details, ask for proof where needed and report suspicious information through the website.
