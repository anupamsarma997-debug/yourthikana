Hotels, Homestays, PGs & Rooms in Itanagar

Itanagar, Arunachal Pradesh — Itanagar is the capital of Arunachal Pradesh and a key urban centre for government, education and business visits. It is a practical starting point for planning travel to other parts of the state.

Choosing an area and accommodation in Itanagar

Choose a property based on the office, campus or transport point you need to reach. If continuing to hill destinations, confirm road conditions, journey time and permits before choosing dates.

Look for hotels for short official visits and homestays, PGs or monthly rooms for longer stays where available. Ask about parking, water supply, power backup and local transport.

What to confirm before contacting a host

  • Exact property address and map location.
  • Actual room photos, room type, bed count and guest capacity.
  • Check-in and check-out time, house rules and ID requirements.
  • Total price, taxes, deposit, extra guest fees and cancellation terms.
  • Facilities such as Wi-Fi, hot water, parking, meals and power backup.
  • For monthly rooms: rent cycle, utilities, security deposit and notice period.

Local travel and planning notes

Verify current entry permit requirements through official sources if applicable. Confirm exact location, check-in time, total price and facilities directly with the host.

Property details, prices and availability are provided by owners and can change. A WhatsApp enquiry is not a confirmed reservation. Keep a written record of the agreed dates, room, total price and payment terms; do not pay until you are satisfied that the property and contact details are genuine.
