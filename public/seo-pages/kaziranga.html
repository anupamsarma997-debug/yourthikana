Kaziranga, Assam — destination guide and stay discovery on YourThikana.

Kaziranga is a well-known wildlife destination in Assam and home to Kaziranga National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The park is especially associated with the greater one-horned rhinoceros and the grassland and wetland habitats of the Brahmaputra floodplain. Visitors plan trips around authorised park experiences, nature and local communities.

When choosing accommodation, consider which park range or entry point suits your itinerary and how you will travel. Ask the host for the exact location, room facilities, check-in details and any services offered. Do not assume that a stay includes park tickets, safari permits, guides or transfers unless the owner confirms this clearly.
