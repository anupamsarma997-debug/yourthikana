How to choose a stay

Start with the town closest to your planned route. In hill and remote areas, check road access, weather, transport and any current permit requirements through official sources. Ask each host for the complete address, actual room photos, guest capacity, check-in rules, facilities and final price.

For PGs and monthly rooms, confirm the rent cycle, security deposit, electricity and water charges, internet, furnishings, notice period and refund terms in writing before paying. Do not assume every listing has been independently inspected; verify details and report suspicious information through the website where available.
