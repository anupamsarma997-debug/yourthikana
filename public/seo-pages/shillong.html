Shillong, Meghalaya — destination guide and stay discovery on YourThikana.

Shillong is the capital of Meghalaya and a popular base for exploring the Khasi Hills. The city is known for its hill setting, music culture, local markets and viewpoints. Places commonly included in a city visit are Ward's Lake, Shillong Peak and the Don Bosco Museum. Travellers also use Shillong as a starting point for trips towards Sohra (Cherrapunji), Dawki and other parts of Meghalaya.

Before choosing accommodation, consider the part of Shillong you need to reach and how you plan to travel. Central areas may suit visitors who want access to shops and restaurants, while other neighbourhoods may be more suitable for a quieter stay. Confirm the full address, road access, check-in arrangements, room capacity and available facilities with the host. Weather and road conditions can affect onward travel, so leave flexibility in your itinerary.
